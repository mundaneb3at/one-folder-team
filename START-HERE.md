# Start here

This page is the whole guide. Do the steps in order. Each one takes a few minutes.
Stop after any step if that is enough for you. Nothing breaks if you stop.

**You need:** a Google account, a phone or computer, and about 30 minutes in total.
**Cost:** free. Every app here has a free plan. Do not pay for anything to follow this page.

---

## Part 1 - Set up (do this once)

### Step 1. Watch or listen (1 minute)
Watch [media/intro-60s.mp4](media/intro-60s.mp4), or listen to [media/intro-60s.mp3](media/intro-60s.mp3).
It shows the idea: one folder, a few helpers, you in charge.

### Step 2. Make your folder (3 minutes)
1. Open Google Drive.
2. Make a new folder. Name it `WORK-HQ`.
3. Inside it, make these folders: `BOOKS`, `CLIENTS`, `IDEAS`, `LOOPS`, `_old`.
4. Inside `BOOKS`, make one more folder: `receipts`.
5. Copy this page into `WORK-HQ` too, so every helper can read the rules.

The full map of the folder is in [DRIVE-LAYOUT.md](DRIVE-LAYOUT.md).

### Step 3. Copy the starter files (3 minutes)
Copy the files from [templates/](templates/) into your folder:
- `TASKS.md` goes in `WORK-HQ`.
- `BOOKKEEPING.md` goes in `BOOKS`.
- `CLIENTS.md` goes in `CLIENTS`.

### Step 4. Pick ONE helper app (5 minutes)
Pick Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. They are the easiest to start with.
Sign up and connect it to your folder. The steps for each app are in [SIGNUP.md](SIGNUP.md).
Only let the app see the `WORK-HQ` folder if it lets you choose. Not your whole Drive. Not your email.
Then make a folder for it: `WORK-HQ/claude/out` (or `chatgpt/out`, or `gemini/out`). It writes only there.

### Step 5. Give it the rules (2 minutes)
1. Copy the **talk-short rule** from [README.md](README.md#the-one-rule-talk-to-me-short).
2. Paste it into the app (its custom instructions, or the first message of a chat).
3. Then paste that app's starter prompt from [prompts/](prompts/).

### Step 6. Let it interview you (10 minutes or more)
Open [INTERVIEW.md](INTERVIEW.md) and paste it into the app.
Talk as long as you like. Voice is fine.
At the end it writes a short task list. Read it. Put the good tasks in `TASKS.md`.

**Set up is done.** You have one folder and one helper that knows your work.

## Part 2 - Use it (every day and every week)

### Step 7. Daily (5 minutes)
1. Snap your receipts into `BOOKS/receipts/` (one folder per month).
2. Tell your helper what you did today, in one or two sentences.
3. Look at `TASKS.md`. Pick tomorrow's **one** most important thing.

### Step 8. Weekly (20 minutes)
1. Ask your helper to draft this week's money lines for `BOOKKEEPING.md`.
2. **Check the numbers yourself.** Helpers make mistakes with money.
3. Ask it for three small ideas for next week. Keep one.

The full set of routines is in [PLAYBOOK.md](PLAYBOOK.md).

## Part 3 - Grow it (only when Part 2 feels easy)

### Step 9. Add a second helper
Pick one more app from [FREE-MODELS.md](FREE-MODELS.md). Give it its own `out` folder.
Ask it to read the first helper's newest file and give a second opinion.
That is how helpers "talk": they leave files, you pick the winner.

### Step 10. Let a helper work through a list
A helper can work through its own to-do file, one item at a time, while you check in.
How to do it safely is in [LOOPS.md](LOOPS.md).

## Add later

These are open slots. When a new part is ready, it goes in its slot as one line and one link.
The steps above do not change.

- **Add later: reminders.** A helper that nudges you at a set time (for example, "send invoices on Friday").
- **Add later: watchers.** A helper that notices when a new file lands in the folder and starts work on it.
- **Add later: hand-offs.** One helper finishes, then passes its file to the next helper on its own.
- **Add later: checkers.** A second helper that checks every money or client file before you read it.
- **Add later: more examples.** One page per kind of business, like [USE-CASE-salon.md](USE-CASE-salon.md).
- **Add later: your own idea.** See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) to suggest a new part.

## If you get stuck

- **A helper gives a long answer.** Paste the talk-short rule again.
- **A helper cannot see your files.** Check the connect steps for that app in [SIGNUP.md](SIGNUP.md).
- **It all feels like too much.** Go back to one helper and Step 7 only. That is a good result.

## Safety, short

- No card numbers, passwords or bank logins in any file.
- Never let a helper pay, send or delete anything without asking you first.
- Client notes: only what you need to do a good job. Ask clients first.
- This kit is not tax, legal or money advice.

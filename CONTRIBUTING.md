# Contributing

Changes go in through a pull request. The owner reviews and merges. Nobody pushes straight to `main`.

## Steps

1. **Make a branch** off `main`. Name it for the change, for example `add-reminders-slot`.
2. **Make your change** on that branch. Keep it small: one idea per branch.
3. **Check it:**
   - every link in the files you touched opens the right file;
   - plain words, short sentences (aim for grade 8);
   - no personal facts: no real names, emails, home paths, school, health or money details.
4. **Open a pull request** into `main`. Say in two or three lines what changed and why.
5. **The owner merges** (or asks for changes). Do not merge your own pull request.

## Adding a new part

New parts plug into the **Add later** list at the end of [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md):

1. Put the new part in its own file (for example `REMINDERS.md`).
2. Change its **Add later** line into one short line plus a link to that file.
3. Add the file to [INVENTORY.md](INVENTORY.md).

Do not rewrite the numbered steps in START-HERE.md to fit a new part. If a part needs a step, add it in Part 3.

# Explore: a mini common space

No orders here. These are small things you could try when you are curious, to get a feel for what the helpers can do.
Each one takes a few minutes. Pick one that sounds fun, see how it feels, and decide for yourself if it is worth keeping.

## Try one bot for a week

- Use only one app for 7 days. Talk to it at the end of each day.
- At the end of the week ask it: "What did you learn about my business? What could you do better next week?"

## Make two bots check each other

- Ask Claude for a price list for a new service.
- Ask Muse or Grok: "Read claude/out/<that file>. What is missing? What would a customer think?"
- You pick what to keep. This is the whole "team" idea in one step.

## Money questions that are fun to ask

- "Which day of the week makes me the most money?"
- "Which product pays for itself fastest?"
- "If I raised one service price a little, how many fewer clients could I lose and still come out ahead?"

The bot works from your `BOOKKEEPING.md`. Check its math with a calculator.

## Client ideas

- A short "welcome back" message for clients you have not seen in 3 months. You send it, not the bot.
- A list of questions to ask new clients at the first visit.
- A "how to get ready for your appointment" note to text people.

## Marketing ideas

- 10 photo post ideas that do not need new photos.
- A reply template for good and bad online reviews.
- What others like you nearby charge (Grok or Manus can look this up; check the sources).

## Make it yours

- Rename the folders. Change the caps in [LOOPS.md](LOOPS.md). Drop apps you do not like.
- If a routine in [PLAYBOOK.md](PLAYBOOK.md) feels like a chore, stop doing it. That is a fine result.
- Add your own idea here. This file is a scratchpad.

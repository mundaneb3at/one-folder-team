# Free AI Assistants: A Plain-Words Catalog

Checked on 2026-11-01. Free plans change often. Anything marked "check current plan" could not be confirmed from the company's own page on that date. Always look at the tool's own settings before you trust a number here.

You do not need all of these. Pick two or three, learn them well, and let them share one Google Drive folder (see the last section).

How to read each block: what it is, good for, free limits, Google Drive, phone app, privacy, sources.

A word on privacy before you start: never paste passwords, bank numbers, or clients' private details into any free assistant. Use first names and rough numbers instead.

---

## 1. ChatGPT (free)

- **What it is:** A general-purpose chat assistant from OpenAI.
- **Good for:**
  - Drafting replies to client messages and booking questions.
  - Writing social posts and short ads.
  - Explaining a receipt or tidying a price list.
- **Free limits:** OpenAI's pricing page (checked 2026-11-01) lists the Free plan as "Unlimited text chats", with the note "subject to abuse guardrails". Uploads, image creation, voice chats and deep research are each listed as "Limited", with no number.
- **Google Drive:** OpenAI's connectors page says "Select plugins are available in all plans, including ChatGPT Free" and "Some plugins require a paid plan". It does not say which group Google Drive is in. Check in Settings. Free workaround: download a file from Drive and upload it into the chat.
- **Voice/phone app:** Yes, iPhone and Android apps. The Free plan lists "Limited voice chats" (no number).
- **Privacy:** Check current plan. Look in Settings, Data controls, for the "improve the model for everyone" switch.
- **Sources (checked 2026-11-01):** https://chatgpt.com/pricing, https://chatgpt.com/features/connectors/ (OpenAI's help center pages returned 403 that day)

## 2. Claude (free)

- **What it is:** A chat assistant from Anthropic, strong at writing and careful reading of long documents.
- **Good for:**
  - Rewriting a rough client email so it sounds kind and clear.
  - Turning notes into a price list or a policy page.
  - Reading a PDF receipt or invoice and listing the totals.
- **Free limits:** Anthropic does not publish a number. Usage is limited per session. Check current plan.
- **Google Drive:** Yes. Anthropic's help page says the Google Drive connector is available to all users on Claude and Claude Desktop. It can search and read Docs, Sheets, Slides, PDFs, and Office files. It can also upload files, create folders, and save files Claude makes to Drive. Sharing, moving, and trashing need your approval. Live editing of Docs and Sheets uses separate connectors. Some free-plan details disagree between sites: check current plan.
- **Voice/phone app:** Phone apps exist. Voice on the free plan: check current plan.
- **Privacy:** Anthropic says that if the training setting is on, free-account chats can be used to train models (kept up to five years). If you turn it off, 30-day retention applies. You can change it at claude.ai/settings/data-privacy-controls. The default setting: check current plan.
- **Sources:** https://support.claude.com/en/articles/10166901-use-google-workspace-connectors, https://www.anthropic.com/news/updates-to-our-consumer-terms

## 3. Google Gemini (free app, and Gemini in Drive/Docs)

- **What it is:** Google's assistant, in the Gemini app and on gemini.google.com.
- **Good for:**
  - Planning a week of social posts with images.
  - Helping with a spreadsheet of expenses or appointments.
  - Quick research on suppliers or local rules (Deep Research is listed in the free plan).
- **Free limits:** Google says your limit "refreshes every five hours until you reach your weekly limit." No exact numbers. Free plan lists a lighter model, image creation, Deep Research, Gemini Live, Canvas, and Gems (your own saved helpers).
- **Google Drive:** Free plan includes 15 GB of Google storage (shared with Gmail and Photos). Google's plan page lists Gemini inside Gmail and Docs only for paid plans. Whether the free Gemini app can read your Drive files: check current plan.
- **Voice/phone app:** Yes. Gemini Live is a voice chat listed in the free plan. Android and iPhone apps.
- **Privacy:** Google says that with "Keep Activity" on, your chats can be used to improve services, including training AI. Turn it off and future chats are not used for training. Some chats are read by human reviewers and kept up to three years, even after you delete. Do not enter confidential info.
- **Sources:** https://gemini.google/subscriptions/, https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961

## 4. Microsoft Copilot (free)

- **What it is:** Microsoft's free consumer assistant, on the web, in Windows, and as a phone app.
- **Good for:**
  - Drafting messages and job ads.
  - Simple "how do I do this in Excel" help.
  - Summarizing a web page about a supplier.
- **Free limits:** Microsoft's support page (checked 2026-11-01): you can "chat with Copilot, create images, upload files and more for free, subject to available capacity and limits. When you hit a limit, you can choose to upgrade to a paid option or come back later." No number is published.
- **Google Drive:** Microsoft's support page lists connectors for OneDrive, Outlook.com, Google Drive, and Gmail/Calendar/Contacts, and says you must connect your Google account yourself. Whether free accounts have it everywhere: check current plan.
- **Voice/phone app:** Yes, with voice. Check current plan for limits.
- **Privacy:** Check current plan. Second-hand sources say uploaded files are not used for training and chat training can be turned off. Verify in Microsoft's privacy settings.
- **Sources (checked 2026-11-01):** https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/learning/changes-microsoft-copilot-app, https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot/connecting-microsoft-copilot-to-other-services (that page lists Microsoft 365 Personal, Family, Premium and Pro, so free accounts may not get Google Drive)

## 5. Meta AI (powered by Muse Spark)

- **What it is:** Meta's free assistant, in the Meta AI app, on meta.ai, and inside Meta's messaging apps.
- **Good for:**
  - Quick caption ideas for Instagram or Facebook.
  - Brainstorming names, offers, and slogans.
  - Voice chat while your hands are busy.
- **Free limits:** Meta's help page "Manage AI usage for your Meta One plan" (read 2026-11-01) says that if you reach your free daily usage limit you can upgrade to a paid Meta One plan or wait for the limit to refresh. No number is given. Everyday chat is described as free.
- **Google Drive:** No Drive connector found. Check current plan. Use copy and paste.
- **Voice/phone app:** Yes. Voice conversation is reported in the Meta AI app.
- **Privacy:** Meta's AI pages say it uses your chats with its AIs to improve AI at Meta. No opt-out was found. It is tied to your Meta accounts.
- **Sources (checked 2026-11-01):** https://www.meta.com/help/artificial-intelligence/1831790234178989/ (Meta), https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/12/meta-ai-app-enhanced-with-new-features-using-muse-spark-heres-whats-new/ (news)

- **Sources (checked 2026-11-01):** https://grok.com/ (no limit text); x.ai/grok, x.ai/plans, help.x.com Grok pages: 403

## 7. DeepSeek

- **What it is:** A chat assistant from a Chinese company, free in an app and on the web.
- **Good for:**
  - Math and step-by-step reasoning (price and margin checks).
  - Drafting plain text.
  - Explaining a spreadsheet formula.
- **Free limits:** DeepSeek's own Terms and Privacy pages (checked 2026-11-01) state no message cap. Users report "server busy" messages at busy times instead of a fixed limit.
- **Google Drive:** None found. Check current plan.
- **Voice/phone app:** App exists. Voice: check current plan.
- **Privacy:** DeepSeek's privacy policy (updated Feb 10, 2026) says it will "directly collect, process and store your Personal Data in People's Republic of China", and uses data "to train and improve our technology". Not for client details.
- **Sources (checked 2026-11-01):** https://cdn.deepseek.com/policies/en-US/deepseek-privacy-policy.html, https://cdn.deepseek.com/policies/en-US/deepseek-terms-of-use.html

## 8. Mistral Le Chat / Vibe

- **What it is:** Mistral is a French company. Its assistant used to be called Le Chat. Mistral's pricing page now presents **Vibe** as "your personal AI agent for everyday tasks" on web and phone. Vibe also names Mistral's coding tools (a terminal agent, and plugins for VS Code and JetBrains). So Vibe is a family: the everyday agent is the one you want.
- **Good for:**
  - Drafting client messages in several languages.
  - Document questions.
  - Light image creation for posts.
- **Free limits:** The free plan lists "limited messages and web searches," limited coding sessions, basic image generation, limited document storage, and 100+ connectors. No numbers. Check current plan.
- **Google Drive:** Free plan lists 100+ connectors, but the page does not name Google Drive for Free. Check current plan.
- **Voice/phone app:** Yes, Vibe on iOS and Android. Voice: check current plan.
- **Privacy:** Page mentions an opt-out for training but does not make clear which plan has what default. Check current plan.
- **Sources:** https://mistral.ai/en/pricing

## 11. Perplexity (free)

- **What it is:** An answer engine: you ask, it searches the web and shows its sources.
- **Good for:**
  - Checking a supplier, a rule, or a local fact with links.
  - Comparing products before you buy.
  - Finding what a competitor charges.
- **Free limits:** Perplexity's plan page (checked 2026-11-01) lists for Free: "Practically unlimited basic searches", "Very limited amount of Pro Searches" and "Basic file uploads (limited)". A plan table was read as 3 Pro Searches a day; not rechecked.
- **Google Drive:** Paid only. Perplexity's page says the Google Drive Connector "is available for Perplexity Pro, Perplexity Max, Enterprise Pro, and Enterprise Max users."
- **Voice/phone app:** Yes, phone apps. Voice: check current plan.
- **Privacy:** Perplexity says AI data retention is on by default for Free. Turn it off in Account settings, Preferences, AI data retention. It only applies to data after you switch it off.
- **Sources (checked 2026-11-01):** https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/11187416-which-perplexity-subscription-plan-is-right-for-you, https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/12870620-connecting-perplexity-with-google-drive, https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/11564572-data-collection-at-perplexity

## 12. NotebookLM (now called Gemini Notebook)

- **What it is:** A Google tool where you load your own documents and ask questions that are answered only from them. Reported renamed to Gemini Notebook in July 2026; the old address still redirects.
- **Good for:**
  - Asking questions of your policies, price lists, and supplier terms.
  - Making a summary or audio overview of a long document.
  - Training a new helper from your own how-to notes.
- **Free limits:** Reported as 100 notebooks, 50 sources each, 50 chats a day (blogs, not Google). Google's page we read says 500,000 words or 200 MB per source. Check current plan.
- **Google Drive:** Yes. Drive files can be added as sources (reported by third parties). Check current plan.
- **Voice/phone app:** Audio overviews are spoken. Phone app: check current plan.
- **Privacy:** Google's page says "Your data will never be shared by Gemini Notebook." Training policy: check current plan.
- **Sources:** https://support.google.com/notebooklm/answer/16269187, https://oit.ncsu.edu/2026/07/17/notebooklm-changes-name-to-gemini-notebook/

## How they share work through one Drive folder

This kit uses one Google Drive folder called `WORK-HQ`. Each app gets its own `out` folder inside it (`claude/out/`, `gemini/out/` and so on) and only writes new plain files there. Shared files like `TASKS.md` belong to you: the apps read them and suggest, you decide. Apps without a Drive connector (see blocks above) do not touch the folder: you paste their answer into their `out` folder yourself. The full layout is in [DRIVE-LAYOUT.md](DRIVE-LAYOUT.md). Keep client private details out of the folder.
